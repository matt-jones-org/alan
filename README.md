# Alan

Alan is an agent that helps with job applications.

Applying for jobs is mostly the same handful of chores repeated for every posting: read the
listing closely, figure out which parts of your background actually match, tailor the resume,
write a cover letter that doesn't sound like every other cover letter, answer the screening
questions, and keep track of where everything stands. Alan does the tedious parts so you can
spend your time on the parts that need you.

## What it does

- **Reads the posting.** Pulls out the requirements, the nice-to-haves, and the things the
  listing cares about but doesn't say outright.
- **Matches it to you.** Works from your real experience and points out where you're a strong
  fit and where there's a gap you should address.
- **Tailors the resume.** Reorders and rewords existing bullets for the role. It doesn't invent
  jobs, titles, dates or skills.
- **Drafts the cover letter and short answers.** A first draft in your voice that you edit, not
  a finished product you send blind.
- **Keeps track.** Which roles you applied to, when, with which version of your resume, and
  what's next.

You review everything before it goes anywhere. Alan never submits an application on its own.

## Status

Early. This repository is where Alan is being built; there's no release yet. The list above is
what it's for, not a claim that every piece is done.
